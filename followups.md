### ⚠️ Three things that fail silently here — do not "simplify" any of them

1. **The sheet reader returns a discriminated union, not `[]`.** The other two readers log-and-return-empty; doing that here would render every date as OPEN and invite people to claim dates already covered — the exact problem the feature exists to prevent. `lib/sheets/coach-meals.ts` returns `{ok:false}` and the page shows an error state instead of availability. Verified by test.
2. **The ten option strings live in three files** (`lib/coach-meals.ts`, `create-coach-meals-form.gs`, `coach-meals-automation.gs`) and must match byte-for-byte. Google *silently ignores* an unmatched prefill value rather than erroring, so drift produces a link that opens the form with nothing ticked and no symptom. **Run `scripts/check-coach-meal-options.py` after touching any of them** — it also enforces the no-comma and ASCII-only rules.
3. **No commas in an option string, ever.** Sheets joins multiple checkbox selections into one cell separated by `", "`, so a comma inside an option makes the cell unsplittable. That is why they read "August 23 - Rudy's BBQ" and not "Sunday, August 23 - ...". The page adds the weekday back for display.
