## Kendra's spec (her asks — do not lose these)

### 1. Outcome
Sponsor-facing form directly on mcneilmavericks.org: a business selects a package and submits info + assets without leaving the site. Club follows up with confirmation + payment instructions.

- "Select This Sponsorship" button under every base level and add-on.
- Preselect the clicked base tier or add-on when the form opens.
- Email each submission to fundraising@mcneilmavericks.org.
- Store every submission in a downloadable administrative list; ideally also send to the committee's shared tracker.
- Ask preferred payment method; show confirmation page + confirmation email explaining an invoice and payment instructions will follow. (Her note: "Or, if you can automate the invoice with a credit card link, that would be better. You decide.")

### 2. Required corrections to the sponsorship page
- **Offer structure:** Blue → MVP as the five base levels + Custom as the final base choice. Tunnel and Scoreboard are optional add-ons, selectable individually or together.
- **Scoreboard price:** $3,000 total for two seasons ($1,500 value/season), paid in full upfront (internal fact — do NOT say "paid upfront" in public benefits text).
- **Game program:** remove "Game program" and "Game program: Full page/Cover ad" from every level.
- **Signs:** Gold and higher include a field sign at all McNeil varsity games and all McNeil home freshman/JV games, plus a sign on McNeil Dr.
- **Scoreboard venue:** scoreboard is at McNeil Stadium; varsity home games are at KRAC, so scoreboard exposure is primarily freshman/JV games at McNeil Stadium. Must be disclosed.
- **CTA:** "Select This Sponsorship" buttons; keep Kendra's email for sponsorship questions.
- **Payment preference:** sponsor selects credit card, Venmo, or check. No payment credentials collected. State that an invoice + instructions will be emailed.
- **Branding:** Lato, navy #011858, green #1E541E, approved horseshoe/collegiate MMFBC logo.

### 4. Form fields and behavior
**A. Selection:** base = single-select (Blue, Gold, Platinum, Diamond, MVP, Custom, "No base package—add-on only"; Custom listed just before add-on-only). Custom selection reveals required open-text: "Tell us about the custom package, idea, or in-kind contribution you would like to discuss." Show price/term/benefits next to each choice. Preselect from the button clicked; base-tier button preselects that tier (changeable); add-on button preselects the add-on and sets base to add-on-only.

**B. Business/contact fields:** Business name (req); Public display name (req — for signs/website/announcements); Contact name + title (req); Email (req); Phone (req, standard formats); Mailing address (req); Website URL (opt); Social handles (opt); Referred by (opt); Add-ons multi-select (opt — Tunnel $350/one season, Scoreboard $3,000/two seasons; either/both/neither); Preferred payment method (req — credit card / Venmo / check, with invoice notice).

**C. Assets:** logo upload PNG/JPG/PDF/SVG/EPS (recommend hi-res transparent PNG or vector). Platinum/Diamond/MVP: request audio-commercial file (MP3/WAV) or "Please contact me for help"; explain approved number of 30-sec spots. Uploads optional at submit — sponsor may email assets by Aug 14, 2026.

### 5. Payment preference / invoice / follow-up
No card/bank/Venmo credentials collected. Notice under payment field: "After you submit your sponsorship selection, MMFBC will email you an invoice and payment instructions for your selected method." Follow-up invoice confirms base + add-ons + total, with instructions for credit card, Venmo (@McNeil-Football), or check. Payment questions → Ashley Root, treasurer@mcneilmavericks.org.
